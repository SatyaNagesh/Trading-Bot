# QuantLab AI — Project Manifesto

> **Who We Are, What We Build, How We Think**  
> Version 1.0 | Last Updated: July 2026

## Our Identity

We are **QuantLab AI** — a quantitative research operating system that amplifies human research capability through artificial intelligence.

We are not a trading bot company. We are not a hedge fund. We are not a get-rich-quick scheme.

We are a **research platform** — a systematic, scientific, and transparent system for discovering, validating, and deploying statistically robust trading strategies.

---

## The Five Principles

### 1. Science First, Trading Second
The primary output of QuantLab AI is **knowledge**, not money. Profit is a byproduct of correctly understanding market microstructure — not the goal itself. Every trade is an experiment, every strategy is a hypothesis, and every loss is data.

### 2. AI Amplifies, Not Replaces
AI agents are research assistants, not replacements. Human judgment provides:
- Strategic direction and market intuition
- Ethical boundaries and risk tolerance
- Interpretation of ambiguous results
- Creative hypothesis generation

AI agents provide:
- Tireless computation and analysis
- Systematic coverage of hypothesis space
- Objective evaluation free from emotional bias
- Memory and pattern recognition at scale

### 3. Modularity Is Freedom
Every component of QuantLab AI is designed to be:
- Independently testable
- Independently replaceable
- Independently improvable
- Independently understandable

This modularity gives us the freedom to evolve any part of the system without rebuilding the whole.

### 4. Rigor Over Speed
We measure our success by the **reliability** of our results, not the speed of our outputs. A single well-validated strategy is worth more than a thousand untested ideas. We optimize for correctness first, performance second.

### 5. Open Systems, Open Minds
QuantLab AI is built on open standards, open protocols, and open architectures. We avoid vendor lock-in, proprietary formats, and black-box dependencies. Our platform should be understandable, extensible, and forkable.

## What We Do NOT Do

We do not:
- Trade based on tips, rumors, or unsolicited information
- Use strategies we cannot explain
- Deploy strategies we have not validated
- Risk capital we cannot afford to lose
- Over-optimize to historical data
- Ignore transaction costs, slippage, or market impact
- Trade illiquid instruments with large capital
- Assume the future will repeat the past
- Hack, exploit, or manipulate markets
- Keep secrets from our stakeholders

---

## Our Relationship With Money

Money is:
- A **measuring stick** for our strategies
- A **constraint** we optimize within
- A **resource** we manage responsibly

Money is NOT:
- Our primary motivation
- A substitute for correct methodology
- Worth pursuing outside our risk parameters
- More important than our principles

## The QuantLab AI Ethos

In three sentences:
1. We form hypotheses about how markets work.
2. We test those hypotheses with scientific rigor.
3. We deploy only what we have proven.

Everything else is noise.
